# GB04 & GB06 — measured, iterated, and the verdict

**Date:** 2026-10-20
**Question:** GB04 (previous-day high/low support/resistance) was rewritten to fix its mechanics —
can any declared configuration clear trading costs? And is GB06 (EMA 9/21 crossover) good enough
to put in the UI?

**Outcome:** No, on both. GB04's best declared setting is **netPF 0.976 at 5 bps** after three
rounds of iteration; GB06 is **0.755** (docs/GB06-COMPLETE-BACKTEST-2026-10-20.md). Neither is
registered as a UI/live engine.

---

## 1. GB04 — what was mechanically wrong, and what changed

Measured 2026-10-20, 40 symbols × 20 sessions, the original engine fired 1,835 signals with
gross PF 0.976 and a hard side split — SHORT (resistance rejection) PF 1.224, LONG (support
bounce) 0.778. Three causes, all silent:

1. **The stop was the bar's wick.** `invalidation = bar.low` on a reclaim bar — a bar that by
   definition has a long wick through the level. Risk was set by the spike, not by the idea, and
   68–75% of trades stopped out.
2. **The target was the nearest structure.** VWAP/POC usually sit closer than the trade pays for,
   so 901 of 1,835 signals died at the fill re-plan as `REFILL_RR`.
3. **The two sides were carried together**, hiding that one of them had no edge.

The rewrite ([strategies/gb04.js](../strategies/gb04.js)) fixes all three, configurable so each
choice is measured rather than asserted:

* stop at the level ± `stopBufferAtr` (not the wick),
* a `confirmAtr` margin so a one-tick poke is not a reclaim,
* targets offered only when they clear `targetR`, plus the measured move (so the RR gate cannot
  be failed by construction),
* `sides` / `vwapFilter` / directional-close switches.

Regression tests: [test/gb04.test.js](../test/gb04.test.js) (6 tests, all passing).

## 2. The measurement — three rounds, one corpus

Corpus `data/ohlcv_1m_full` · 1,031 symbols · 60 sessions (2026-07-13 → 2026-10-06) · next-open
fill · hold 90 bars · cost ladder 0/0.02/0.05/0.10%. The cost hurdle at 5 bps is ~0.22R per
trade (measured on GB06's book: 0.05% round trip ÷ average risk).

**Round 1** (10 declared variants, both sides): every setting loses; best 0.868 netPF. The only
profitable pocket: resistance-rejection SHORT, gross PF 1.199 (+0.12R/trade).

**Round 2** (trend/ATR filters on the SHORT pocket): VWAP alignment does not help (0.815–0.869);
an ATR 0.20% floor reaches **0.919**.

**Round 3** ([scratch/gb04_grid3.js](../scratch/gb04_grid3.js), 12 declared variants — stop
width, per-day caps, morning window, R3 targets, shorter hold):

| variant | trades | win% | netPF @5bps |
|---|---|---|---|
| SHORT anchor ATR.20 | 6,834 | 39.3% | 0.919 |
| SHORT stopBuf .40 | 10,244 | 40.1% | 0.917 |
| SHORT stopBuf .55 | 10,645 | 40.1% | 0.929 |
| **SHORT ATR.20 + stopBuf .40** | 7,127 | 39.8% | **0.976** |
| SHORT ATR.30 | 4,239 | 38.4% | 0.944 |
| SHORT max1/day | 7,566 | 39.1% | 0.868 |
| SHORT morning ≤11:30 | 6,104 | 39.1% | 0.905 |
| SHORT R3 targets | 7,410 | 34.0% | 0.950 |
| SHORT ATR.20, hold 45 | 6,834 | 39.7% | 0.908 |
| LONG ATR.20 | 6,160 | 32.3% | 0.687 |
| both ATR.20 | 12,641 | 35.9% | 0.800 |

The whole parameter family lands between 0.69 and 0.98 — the spread across variants is smaller
than the distance to break-even, which is what "no edge after costs" looks like. The stop-width
result is the interesting near-miss: a wider stop shrinks cost-as-a-fraction-of-R and lifts the
win rate, and 0.976 is the closest any honest setting gets, but it is still under 1.00.

## 3. GB06 — measured the same day

Fixed and measured in [docs/GB06-COMPLETE-BACKTEST-2026-10-20.md](GB06-COMPLETE-BACKTEST-2026-10-20.md):
46,696 trades, gross PF 1.051, **0.755 at 5 bps**; every SL/TP ratio in a declared grid lands
0.755–0.810. Registered in the backtest registry (tests pin its three former defects), not armed
live, not in the UI's measured-engine line.

## 4. Verdict

* **Do not register GB04 or GB06 as UI/live engines.** The standing gate is netPF ≥ 1 after
  stated costs on a real sample (and ≥ 1.5 with ≥ 30 trades to *speak*); neither reaches even
  the first line after three rounds of iteration on each.
* This repeats yesterday's finding on the old GB04
  ([docs/BACKTEST-GB04-GB01S-GB03S-2026-10-19.md](BACKTEST-GB04-GB01S-GB03S-2026-10-19.md))
  with a mechanically corrected engine: the leak was never just the wick-stop. A previous-day
  level reclaim on 1-minute bars has ~+0.12R gross edge on the short side against ~0.22R of
  cost.
* The honest next lever is not another filter on this entry — it is a cheaper exit (partial
  profits / breakeven stop management) or a slower timeframe where the level reclaim is not
  competing with the spread on every trade.

## 5. Reproducing

```bash
node scratch/gb04_grid.js  --days=60      # rounds 1–2 variants
node scratch/gb04_grid3.js --days=60      # round 3 variants (needs --max-old-space-size=8192)
node --test test/gb04.test.js test/gb06.test.js
```

Note: `--days=60` is not cosmetic — an unbounded run retains every session in the corpus and
exits with a V8 heap OOM around symbol 576.
