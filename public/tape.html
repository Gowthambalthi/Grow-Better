One row per symbol, folded from the raw captured ticks — price now, the price 10s and 20s ago, the volume actually traded inside the window (the tape carries cumulative day volume, so the window's volume is last − first, never the running total), and both sides of the book the capture records. Quote imb is the LEVEL-1 RESTING quote (size on the bid vs size on the ask) and Day buy / sell is the feed's own day totals (totalBuyQuantity / totalSellQuantity), named for what the tape records rather than called aggressor flow — the packet does not carry which side TRADED. A negative spread means the feed sent a crossed book (bid > ask); it is shown, not hidden. This page reads recorded ticks; it cannot place an order or name a direction.
